# AI_ProgressTracking.md

## Purpose

- Append-only history of THIS project: what was planned, built, decided and verified, in order.
- Current state lives in `AI_CarryOn.md` (rewritten in place); this file is never edited after the fact.
- Conventions: `AI_Standards.md` §1.2.

## Logging Rules

- Append after **each meaningful planning or implementation update** — not once a day, not per commit.
- **Newest at the bottom.**
- ⚠️ Get the real clock before writing a timestamp: `Get-Date -Format "yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm:ss K"` (Windows
  PowerShell) or `date "+%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S %z"`. Never estimate or infer a time.
- Quote Ote's decisions verbatim, with the date.
- `Next action:` is the most-read field — write the next ACTION, not a wish.
